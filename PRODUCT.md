# PRODUCT.md - Napkin Design Co.

Inferred from the client's own build brief and content inventory (init ran without an interview; assumptions labeled).

## What it is
A two-to-three person web studio that hand-codes websites and runs the SEO behind them for small businesses. No page builders, no contracts, clients own everything. The name is the mechanism: businesses start as a sketch on a napkin; Napkin takes them from that sketch to ranking on Google.

## Audience
Small-business owners (trades, photographers, local services). Not designers, not procurement panels. They decide fast, trust receipts over awards, and act by sending an email. Scene (assumption): browsing at night after close, laptop at the kitchen table.

## The action
One conversion: email contact@napkindesign.co. The /contact route does not exist yet, so every CTA is a mailto. One intent label everywhere: "Start a project".

## Real proof (the only claims allowed)

- Average position 7 for "real estate photographer near me" over the last 30 days.
- Achieved in under 4 months with zero backlink budget.

## Brand commitments (pinned by the client brief; these beat any skill default)
- Concept: napkin → glass. Rough resolves into refined; hand-drawn SVG strokes become glass surfaces.
- Tokens: ink-void #0A0A0B ground, napkin #F2EFE6 type, ballpoint #2B4DE0 accent. (Redesign narrows UI accent to ballpoint only; #FF4A24 lives in the keycap imagery, not in UI.)
- Type: Archivo (variable, wdth axis carries display duty). Mono only for real data.
- Assets: logomark + wordmark SVGs; seven 3D keycap renders (the client's own product imagery). The LETS GO keycap is the final CTA.
- Signature interaction: visitors can sketch on the page in ballpoint; the stroke resolves to glass.
- Voice: plain, a little dry, receipts over adjectives. Tagline: "sketch it. ship it. rank it."
- SEO non-negotiable: all copy in real HTML at load, one h1, works without JS. Anchor ids stay stable.
